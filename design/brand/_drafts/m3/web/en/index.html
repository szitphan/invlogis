The problem

Every shipment passes through many papers — and gets re-typed many times.

One container number shows up in dispatch, workshop and receivables. Each place types it once; miss a port deadline and storage fees follow.

  • DEM

    Container storage fees

    Import containers that sit in the port past the free period incur container storage charges.

  • DET

    Empty-return fees

    Counted from the time on the EIR at pick-up until the empty is returned to the place the carrier names; past the limit, fees apply.

  • Paper documents

    Paper arrives late

    EIRs, handover records and weigh slips arrive late, so closing the transport order and costing slip too.

  • Re-typing

    Excel, Zalo, paper

    One shipment's data sits with many people in many files; jobs go out over Zalo and are hard to trace.

How it works

Three steps, one set of data.

  1. 1

    Document in

    Upload it or snap it on a phone. Booking Notes, eDOs, EIRs, weigh slips, declarations with a QR.

  2. 2

    AI reads

    Invlogis reads each field, labels what it read, and points out what needs a second look.

  3. 3

    You review, data moves on

    Fix anything, then save. The same data runs on into dispatch, workshop and receivables — no re-typing.

Platform

One platform, many modules, one set of data.

Reading documents with AI is our strongest point, but not the whole product. The rest is what a trucking business needs every day.

  • AI

    AI document reading

    Customer documents land in the shipment file, so there is less re-typing. You see which fields were read and which need checking before you save.

    • [Booking Note]
    • [eDO]
    • [EIR]
    • [Weigh slip]
    • [QR declaration]

  • Dispatch

    Assign trucks and drivers to port deadlines; see storage and empty-return limits coming so fees are avoided. For ePort ports, keep the ePort task list and its results.

  • Workshop

    Repairs and workshop stock per truck, on the same vehicle data.

  • Vehicles

    Truck records, handover and return; links dispatch with the workshop.

  • Receivables

    Costs and revenue tied to the right shipment for statements and receivables.

  • Driver app

    Receive jobs, photograph documents, log costs on site; keeps working offline.

  • Per-customer setup

    Parameters, names, lists and plan toggles — no custom code per customer.
