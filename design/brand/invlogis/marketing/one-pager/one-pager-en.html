Open a new way to operate.

Invlogis reads Vietnamese shipping documents straight into the shipment file, so you re-type less and your data carries on from port and yard to receivables.

The problem

  • Miss a port deadline and fees follow: demurrage (DEM) when import containers sit past the free period, detention (DET) when the empty comes back late.
  • EIRs, handover records and weigh slips arrive late, so closing the order and costing run late too.
  • One shipment's data sits with many people in many files; jobs go out over Zalo and are hard to trace.

AI that reads Vietnamese documents

Photograph or upload a document. Invlogis reads the key fields into the shipment file; you review and approve before it is saved.
